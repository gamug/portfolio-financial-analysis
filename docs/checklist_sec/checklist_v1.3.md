## Ground rules

1. **Every rule cites a source document**, with a verbatim quote and the PDF page.
   - Our data and code never justify a rule. They only show *that* a case occurs (prevalence, defects), or that a
     rule **cannot be executed** with the data the pipeline captures. The note says which. Coverage counts are given for
     the pilot (449 filings) **and** production (5,075 filings, read-only, checksum `5c5c0642…` unchanged).
   - A point with no source is **not** a rule. It is a **declared limitation** (section L) or a **calibrated
     screen** (Annex A).
2. **Quotes and pages are checked by script** (`scripts/sec_xcheck/quotecheck.py docs/checklist_sec/checklist_v1.3.md`) against the files. Annex B's quotes are checked the same way: this draft with `sec_xcheck/quotecheck_v13.py` (a local extension); the tracked script gains the Annex B rows when v1.3 is tracked.
   Pages are PDF pages. NotebookLM's page numbers were wrong in about a third of the cases, and in one round it
   supplied definitions that are in none of the sources, so nothing is taken from it unchecked.
3. **Counts are generated by script** (section "Totals").
4. **Severity:** **BLOCK** — the value must not be used. **WARN** — used, with a flag. **INFO** — a diagnostic, or a
   documented rule the pipeline cannot execute today (the note says why).
5. **Inference.** Where a rule combines sources, applies a filer rule to the consumer side, or extends a documented
   case by analogy, the note says **"Inference"**.

## Scope (decided)

- **As originally reported.** Values are as first filed, used from their availability date (`available_at`, PER-01).
  Latest-reported values (amendments, restatements, the `frames` API) are out of scope.
- **The company-level DATA_QUALITY veto is kept** (decision 2026-10-07): a HARD data-quality gate excludes the company
  until its data is fixed. A cycle policy, recorded here and not numbered.
- **Extreme values in cross-sectional use are winsorized at 0.5% per tail** (DQ-02).
- **A missing value is never imputed**, neither as 0 nor as a neutral score (MET-00).
- **Company types are overlays.** A metric's status is the most restrictive over the company's active overlays:
  NA > ADJ > A.
- **Ranking is across the whole universe, not within sectors** (VALORIZATION ranks every asset together). Every NA or
  ADJ in the matrix, and every limitation that affects one kind of company more than another (leases, L-01), therefore
  shifts cross-sector comparisons. The thesis declares this **once, in general**, not per bias. How financial firms are
  scored when most VALORIZATION factors are NA for them belongs with `T-071` (sector-specific factors).
- **Three windows** (D-10). *Filings:* filed from 2022 (stored: 2022-01-06 to 2026-09-02). *Fiscal years:* from FY2019,
  because the first 10-Ks (filed early 2022) carry three fiscal years and the CAGR starts at the earliest (PER-04).
  *Taxonomy releases:* 2020–2026 (ID-20; the DQC element lists are pinned for the same window).
- **Negative equity is not a data error** (D-04). APP-07 makes ROE and D/E not applicable, and nothing more: no
  data-quality veto rests on the sign of equity. A distress screen for negative-equity companies is a **cycle policy**,
  outside this checklist: it reads the equity sign, interest coverage and net debt/EBITDA directly; EBITDA ≤ 0 counts
  as distress; it does not apply to financial firms; its thresholds are calibrated, declared with a sensitivity
  analysis and the veto count by sector.
- **Cycle rules are outside this checklist** (`NEGATIVE_FCF`, `LEVERAGE_EXTREME`, `LIQUIDITY_DISTRESS`,
  `PRICE_CRASH`), but their exclusions are reported **by sector**, because a cluster biases the sample. `T-147`'s
  replay: HARD vetoes reach 22 to 26 of the 31 utilities in every cycle, almost all through `NEGATIVE_FCF`.
- **Regulated utilities (D-13, decided 2026-10-09).** GICS Electric, Gas, Multi- and Water Utilities are exempt
  from `NEGATIVE_FCF`: negative free cash flow is the sector's normal state, funded by debt and equity (C13 p.11,
  p.19; C14 pp.38, 40; C15 p.2). A HARD veto applies instead when the **three-year average** of cash flow from
  operations before working-capital changes / total debt is below 5%, the lower bound of the Ba band (5%–13%) of
  Moody's scorecard (C13, PDF p.7, a table that is an image: read by eye, confirmed independently by the reviewer
  against a public copy, crop saved as `C13_p7_scorecard_financial_strength.png`). Moody's metrics are "typically
  calculated based on an average of the most recent three years of reported results" (C13 p.21). Each year's ratio
  uses only data public on D: the latest 10-K's three years of cash flow, and the third year-end debt from the
  previous 10-K. Working capital is current items only; regulatory assets and liabilities stay in the cash flow
  (C13 p.14). Missing inputs: no veto, flagged.
  **Declared inferences:** (a) the debt is MET-07's, not Moody's adjusted debt (which may, for example, exclude
  securitization debt), so the band is applied to a different definition; (b) Water Utilities are included by
  analogy: C13 p.3 rates regulated water companies under a separate methodology, and C15, a water and gas utility,
  shows the same pattern; (c) the sub-industry is a proxy for "rate-regulated" (C13 p.2): CEG and VST are mainly
  unregulated generators that GICS files under Electric Utilities, and AES and NRG (Independent Power Producers)
  are not exempt. **Declared asymmetry:** other sectors are vetoed on any negative free cash flow; utilities only at
  a distress level.
  **Measured** (single year, 28 utilities' latest 10-Ks): 20 of the 25 evaluable would fire `NEGATIVE_FCF`; on the
  replacement none is below 5% (12.2% to 47.7%), so **any threshold from 5% to 12% gives the same result**: that is
  the sensitivity analysis. The three-year average is measured again on the historical universe before the
  thesis.
- **Financial firms: open decision** (D-07). Either excluded explicitly (Fama and French, C4 p.3) or kept with
  sector-specific factors (`T-071`). Measured inputs: about 7.5% of the market capitalization; 12 to 15 of the 47 to 51 evaluated are
  hard-vetoed per cycle (on 2026-09-22: 14 of 51, 10 of them because a bank's revenue does not resolve (APP-01), a resolver defect).
- **Metric formulas: Annex B.** Each metric's formula has one source, is an inference from one, or is marked
  "source pending"; a pending formula is resolved before the thesis-annex version is generated.
